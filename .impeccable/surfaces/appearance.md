Mode: Operate. Extends the owner-pinned Avalon system and the supplied luminous avatar family.

THESIS: Change the visible form while keeping the same mind and active conversation.
OWN-WORLD: Shared real-time GPU rendering: cyan/gold Ethereal Human, violet Neon Sentinel and purple orbital Abstract Core use procedural volumes. Only a face, two tiny hands and two tiny feet; side spheres are eyes, white slashes are the nose, zigzag is the mouth. No torso, body, chest or neck. Clean field without background stars; preserve character texture and cyan/gold flowing rays.
STORY: See the four available forms, select one, then adjust detail, frame limit and motion when needed.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Four large original renderer captures with explicit selected state, followed by labeled detail/frame/motion controls. Two columns on narrow screens.
FORM: Code-led extension of the approved Operate control center; supplied concept is the avatar direction reference rather than a literal app layout replacement.
FINISH: Preserve existing tokens, native typography and focus styles. Persistent appearance changes never reconnect or cancel the runtime. Static fallback and reduced motion retain the avatar/state meaning.

Motion thesis: the single focal avatar breathes subtly and energy filaments circulate. Progre retains its owner-reference face anatomy under texture animation; procedural avatars retain their volume geometry. Light changes with verified work/completion/failure states. Controls acknowledge selection immediately. GPU work is capped by the selected frame limit and sample quality; hidden/locked rendering pauses and still mode draws only on change. Listening, speech level and camera gaze follow actual device state. Native extension JavaScript requires normal logout/login; still captures do not prove live behavior.
